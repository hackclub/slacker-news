Vercel has been a long time partner with Hack Club, and now they’re opening up a direct line with Hack Clubbers! If you are part of the Hack Club Slack, you can now reach out to Vercel directly in the <SlackChannel id="mitochondria-is-the-powerhouse-of-the-vercell" /> channel.

Just some ground rules for the channel:
- This is not meant to be a support channel for Vercel. If you have questions about your account, billing, or other issues, please [reach out to Vercel support directly](https://vercel.com/help).
- The Vercel team members will be in the channel to answer questions, but please be patient if they don’t respond immediately. They are really busy people!
- Vercel might also ask for feedback on any new features they are working on, so the suggestions you make in the channel could help directly shape the future of Vercel!

To make sure you understand these little house keeping rules, Prometheus will ask you to confirm that you understand the purpose of the channel by writing out a short message in the channel that goes along the lines of "I understand the purpose of this channel" once you join. This is just to make sure that everyone is on the same page and that the channel stays high quality!

If all this sounds like fun, please join the Hack Club Slack and head over to the <SlackChannel id="mitochondria-is-the-powerhouse-of-the-vercell" /> channel to say hi to the Vercel team!
