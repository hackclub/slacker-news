Prox2 vanished from Hack Club on September 1st. Since then, <SlackChannel id="confessions" /> has been full of normal humans and there has been a lack of anonymous posts in <SlackChannel id="meta" /> too! The community has been asking for answers for weeks, and we finally have one.

CRT as it was is no more. <SlackMention name="Jeremy" id="U06UYA5GMB5" /> and I have been working to rework how the CRT works at a fundamental level. You may have many questions, so here are a few I can answer now:

**Why?**

And then comes Z! Jokes aside, CRT has been a great thing that has served us for quite some time, but it badly needed some rganizational and technical improvements. The old CRT was fragmented, and it was hard to get things done. For example, CRT ran applications for new members on July 21st, but nothing has happened with them since. This new CRT has a bit more structure and should keep things moving along.

**How secure is this thing?**

The bot has two modes to send confessions. One that is tied to a hashed and salted version of your Slack ID and one that is tied to a generated key. To quote the README:

> the submission form lets you choose a salted account hash or a generated private reply key. neither mode stores your raw Slack user ID. the private key mode needs both the key and the original account for replies, reactions, and withdrawal. you gotta save the key when it is shown, if it is lost, then its gg.
> 
> salted account hashes recognize your account automatically, but in theory, someone with database access can try to crack them against known Slack user IDs. however, the owl bot running in hack club is hosted by trusted members of the community and less people have access to the owl database than the original prox2 database!

**How do I use the new bot?**

You can use the bot by running `/owl` or DMing the bot directly: [redirects to Owl's profile](https://hackclub.slack.com/app_redirect?app=A0C5L27PB9P)

**But this new team is led by people who work closely with HQ! How are you going to make sure that the CRT is independent?**

Good point! In the interest of full disclosure, Jeremy and I (Rowan) are both part of HQ. We both get paid by HQ for the work we do for it. However, we are not going to let that affect how the CRT operates. The CRT is a community team and it will be run by the community. We both have a history of not letting our other positions affect how we act in the community. Personally, I have been doing work for HQ for quite some time and yet I still call them out. I will continue to do so as part of the CRT. Jeremy has a similar history and I trust him to do the same.

**A new bot? Where is the source code?**

Glad you asked! You can find it at [hackclub-community/owl](https://github.com/hackclub-community/owl). Anyone is welcome to submit pull requests and I will try to review them regularly.

**Where is the bot hosted?**

The bot is hosted on a server from [Njalla](https://njal.la/), a trusted privacy-focused hosting provider. Only I (Rowan) and Jeremy have access to the server. The bot will never be hosted on infrastructure that is owned, paid for, or controlled by HQ.

For comparison, the old Prox2 database (aka where all the confessions were stored) was hosted on a DigitalOcean droplet that was paid for by HQ as part of the long gone hack-as-a-service project. HQ had full access to the server at all times. Owl will be more independent than Prox2 ever claimed to be.

**What about the old confessions?**

Ani and I have copies of the old database in case they are needed. However, we do not intend to restore old confessions. Any confessions made via Prox2 cannot be replied to. If you submitted a confession to Prox2 during the downtime, it is lost.

**How can I join the new CRT?**

We are not looking to bring on new people right now. We will mostly be pulling in the existing CRT members who would like to join. However, applications are still open, and we will keep them in case we decide to bring on new people. Do not treat this as a promise that the same application will be used in the future; we may change this.

**Conclusion**

Sorry the wait took so long. Confessions is such a great part of the community and it's awesome to bring it back. We will be tweaking the bot and the team over time, so if you have any feedback, please let us know in <SlackChannel id="confessions-meta" />. Enjoy <Emoji name="ohneheart" />
