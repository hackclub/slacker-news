There are a lot of Hack Clubbers in <SlackChannel id="ship" /> building and sharing amazing projects. Many people put real energy and care into both the project itself and how they present to the community. But in a channel where so much low-effort posting can happen, resources have only ever been devoted to fighting against the burial of quality posts in slop, while not much has been tried in encouraging this exchange of inspiration.

A variety of factors can lead to posting in <SlackChannel id="ship" /> feeling discouraging for the people who are setting an example for the rest of the community, leading to a spiral of losing high quality posters and feedback-givers and <SlackChannel id="ship" /> slowly becomes a less inspiring place for everyone. We want to put a stop to that.

So starting today, HQ will officially be reinvesting in making sure that those who build and share inspiring things are appreciated for their contributions to the community as <SlackChannel id="ship" /> Artifacts of Amazement are officially introduced! Roughly once a month, we, the channel’s stewards (<SlackMention name="kman" id="U091PCWGNBX" />, <SlackMention name="natey" id="U07ET4C0L90" />, <SlackMention name="ttf" id="U093XFSQ344" />, <SlackMention name="Pratham" id="U097N0AKR6Z" />, <SlackMention name="neven" id="U071JHBEJ7R" />, and I), will handpick a few standout projects whose makers who will be surprised with a small thank-you in the mail.

The idea is based on the Ship of the Week challenge that ran during Stardance, which ended up being a great way to highlight some of the best projects in the community but also seemed to successfully renew interest in the components of sharing and participating that makes <SlackChannel id="ship" /> so special. Since Stardance has now come to a close, we wanted to bring that magic back, and with <SlackMention name="eps" id="U09Q8MLTE58" />’s new channel funding experiment, we have the resources to make the rewards for posting and improving the channel more tangible than ever before.

## What winners get

Prizes will include staples like stickers and postcards, but we'll also experiment with merch such as Hack Club shirts, pins, socks, and more. Selected shippers will also still get the chance to be [featured](https://news.hackclub.com/slack/ship-of-the-week/) in Slacker News[^1].

While customs charges unfortunately won’t be covered, we hope that the added incentives are diverse enough to offer something to everyone and will help inspire you to share the insane things you’re already building.

## A token of our appreciation

We know that gameable metrics can be tiring, and that a lot of people are frustrated with systems that end up rewarding whoever optimizes the numbers best. So we’re intentionally carving out a space that’s more organic. As stewards we also want to make it clear that the Artifacts of Amazement aren’t a YSWS or a contest you “win”, and rewards won’t be reserved for groundbreaking projects that break reality (Bad Apple on a piece of toast, anyone?).

If you want predictability and a rubric, YSWS prizes are still there for that. This is simply another option, with a bit more spontaneity and good judgment. We’re intentionally avoiding rankings or leaderboards, and instead hope that the chance of being greeted with something special in the mail might be a good way to encourage people to keep showing up, sharing the coolest stuff they can, and giving the best feedback they can.

Every project that gets shared, and every shipper who follows the <SlackChannel id="ship" /> guidelines, helps set a higher standard for what the channel should look like and promotes the goal of making the Hack Club community and Slack more than just [“not bad”](https://news.hackclub.com/news/new-code-of-conduct/).

We hope you’ll keep sharing what you build in <SlackChannel id="ship" /> and we can't wait to see what you'll make.

[^1]: Note that improvements to ship ads are underway and placement of ship ads earned through community service like this will likely be more prominent.
