Hack Club's <SlackChannel id="ship" /> channel is trying something new: funding the makers behind its best projects. The experiment, called <SlackChannel id="ship" /> Artifacts of Amazement, revives the Ship of the Week challenge from Stardance but with added incentives!

During Stardance, the community ran a "ship of the week," and it turned out to be a really awesome experiment. We wanted to bring it back, but with <SlackMention name="eps" id="U09Q8MLTE58" />'s new channel funding experiment we wanted to make the rewards for posting and improving the channel more tangible.

![Prometheus's pinned announcement in #ship: "yall have been building INSANE projects and showing them off so we thought it was time to celebrate some of the awesome shippers: introducing #ship Artifacts of Amazement!" It says channel stewards will handpick a few standout projects every month or so and send the makers something as a thank-you, that this is not a competition, that Hack Club won't cover customs, and that guidelines apply.](/images/ship-artifacts-of-amazement-announcement.png)

So starting today HQ will be reinvesting in making sure that those who build and share inspiring things are appreciated for this contribution to the commununity. Roughly once a month, the channel's stewards will handpick a few standout projects whose makers will be surprised with a small thank-you.

## What winners get

Prizes will include staples like stickers and postcards, but also merch such as Hack Club t-shirts, pins, socks, and more. Selected shippers will also still get the chance to be [featured](https://news.hackclub.com/slack/ship-of-the-week/) in Slacker News[^1].

While customs charges unfortunately won't be covered, we hope that the added incentives are diverse enough to offer something to everyone, will help inspire you to share the insane things you're already building.

## Appreciation, not a prize

As stewards we also want to make it clear that the Artifacts of Amazement aren't a YSWS or a contest you "win". They also won't be exclusively reserved for groundbraking projects that shatter reality or anything (Doom running on WHOOP anyone?). We're intentionally avoiding implementing rankings or leaderboards and instead simply hope that the chance of being greeted with something special in the mail might be a good way to encourage people to keep showing up in contributing to the community through posting and discussing projects.

Every project that gets shared, and every maker who follows the <SlackChannel id="ship" /> guidelines, helps set the standard for what the channel should look like and avances the overall mission of the Hack Club Slack and community being greater than just ["not bad"](https://news.hackclub.com/news/new-code-of-conduct/).

So keep shipping, keep sharing, and keep following the <SlackChannel id="ship" /> guidelines.  We can't wait to see what you'll make and learn.

[^1]: Note that improvements to ship ads are underway and placement of ship ads earned through community service like this will likely be more prominant.
