Hack Club's <SlackChannel id="ship" /> channel is trying something new: funding the makers behind its best projects. The experiment, called <SlackChannel id="ship" /> Artifacts of Amazement, revives a tradition from Stardance.

During Stardance, the community ran a "ship of the week," and it turned out to be a really awesome experiment. Now that it's back, the channel wants to bring it to <SlackChannel id="ship" />.

Announcing the program, the channel said members have been "building INSANE projects and showing them off." Roughly once a month, the ship stewards will handpick a few standout projects and send their makers a small thank-you.

## What winners get

Prizes include a Hack Club t-shirt, stickers, and more. Shirts and shipping are covered, though customs fees are not.

Selected makers will also get the chance to be featured in Slacker News, so a great project could end up on this page.

## Appreciation, not a prize

The stewards were careful about the framing: this isn't a contest you "win." Nobody is being ranked against anyone else. Instead, the stewards describe the prizes as a token of appreciation for the people who make the channel better. Every project that gets shared, and every maker who follows the <SlackChannel id="ship" /> guidelines, helps set the standard for what the channel looks like.

So there's nothing to compete for. Keep shipping, keep sharing, and keep following the <SlackChannel id="ship" /> guidelines.
