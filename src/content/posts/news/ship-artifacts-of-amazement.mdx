Hack Club's <SlackChannel id="ship" /> channel is trying something new: funding the makers behind its best projects. The experiment, called <SlackChannel id="ship" /> Artifacts of Amazement, revives the Ship of the Week challenge from Stardance but with added incentives!

During Stardance, the community ran a "ship of the week," and it turned out to be a really awesome experiment. We wanted to bring it back, but with @eps's new channel funding experiment we wanted to make the rewards for posting and improving the channel more tangible.

![Prometheus's pinned announcement in #ship: "yall have been building INSANE projects and showing them off so we thought it was time to celebrate some of the awesome shippers: introducing #ship Artifacts of Amazement!" It says channel stewards will handpick a few standout projects every month or so and send the makers something as a thank-you, that this is not a competition, that Hack Club won't cover customs, and that guidelines apply.](/images/ship-artifacts-of-amazement-announcement.png)

So starting today HQ will be reinvesting in making sure that those who build and share amazing things are appreciated for their contributions to the commununity. Announcing the program, the channel said members have been "building INSANE projects and showing them off." Roughly once a month, the ship stewards will handpick a few standout projects and send their makers a small thank-you.

## What winners get

Prizes include a Hack Club t-shirt, stickers, and more. Shirts and shipping are covered, though customs fees are not.

Selected makers will also get the chance to be featured in Slacker News, so a great project could end up on this page.

## Appreciation, not a prize

The stewards were careful about the framing: this isn't a contest you "win." Nobody is being ranked against anyone else. Instead, the stewards describe the prizes as a token of appreciation for the people who make the channel better. Every project that gets shared, and every maker who follows the <SlackChannel id="ship" /> guidelines, helps set the standard for what the channel looks like.

Keep shipping, keep sharing, and keep following the <SlackChannel id="ship" /> guidelines.
