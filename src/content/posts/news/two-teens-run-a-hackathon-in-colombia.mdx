[Macondo](https://macondo.hackclub.com/) was a Hack Club program where teenagers designed and shipped personal projects, in hardware, software or both. Hack Club funded hardware parts up to $1,000, and every hour of building earned "fruits", which could be traded for 3D printers, laptops, tools or a free plane ticket to Bogotá.

The program ended with a free three-day hackathon in Bogotá, Colombia, from August 14 to 16. This video follows two teens as they run it. Watch it above or on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZMHP1OyUOq0).
