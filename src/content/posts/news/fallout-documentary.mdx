Fallout gave students an opportunity to design something that they could make real. In return, 200 of them were invited to a free week-long hardware hackathon in the electronics capitol of the world.

Hack Club has now published a two-part documentary about how that went. Anyone interested in how a program like this happens, or how they too could be given the resources could to learn and build awesome projects should give it a watch.
