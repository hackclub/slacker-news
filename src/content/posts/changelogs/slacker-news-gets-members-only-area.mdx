You deserve better than to have your idea buried in a thread with 1000+ replies. And you deserve a fair hearing. Slacker News is the way. Few responses written carefully, instead of many responses quickly."

As of last week, some articles on Slacker News are hidden unless a user is logged in. This article serves to explain the background behind this change and the reasons behind it.

**Why are we doing this?**

The story of this feature suggestion goes back pretty far, and as far as I know started with a PR <SlackMention name="sahil" id="U08PUHSMW4V" /> submitted to Slacker News when it was still a static jekyll blog. At the time we opted against creating the impression of security for a project which contained raw article markdown in the codebase.

Nevertheless, the motivation behind adding authentication remained relevant until <SlackMention name="ani" id="U01D9DWGEB0" /> proposed an authgate for the site in <SlackChannel id="meta" />. Opinions were variable on how this could be implemented (and whether publishing opinions on SN was even the right path), but a consensus was reached that an authgate would be beneficial to the site's purpose for community opinion and criticism.

Even back then, I was already working on protecting articles, but my chosen method, a self-hosted Payload CMS instance, was complex and had several major flaws (namely performance, stability, and maintainability). While a more seamlessness submission process is still a priority today, we've always known that the comfort and freedom of expression offered to article-submitters was worth it.

But today we're happy to finally announce this second objective as achieved, and that this progress did not require shipping an ineffective protection, close-sourcing the project, or undermining its ability to serve as a place where Hack Clubbers share what they're doing, thinking, and making with each other (and the world[^1]!).

From now on, any published opinion or essay on SN can be designated by its author as intended to be protected, a decision which will place it behind a Hack Club Auth-wall and restrict access to site visitors and RSS feed subscribers with valid authentication verification.

In order to showcase the new auth-gating features of the site, the [Ship Ads](https://news.hackclub.com/slack/ship-of-the-week/) column also temporarily requires authentication to view.
